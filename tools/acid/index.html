      ACIDFLOWER

      How it plays

      • Bass line: each note sits at its pitch, like a piano roll: drag it up or down to change it; tap it to rest it. Oct lifts it an octave, then drops it; Acc accents it (louder, a sharper squelch); Slide glides it into the next note.
      • Tools: Key transposes the bass, Len sets the pattern’s length. Clear asks for a second tap.
      • Drums: tap a step to set or clear it, or drag across steps to paint. Tap a drum’s name to hear it.
      • Squelch pad: cutoff left to right, resonance bottom to top. The strips do the rest: drag up or down.
      • Punch: hold a pad for an effect on the whole mix, let go to drop out; hold several at once. Repeats start on the beat. Latch makes a tap turn an effect on and another turn it off. In rave mode they run along the bottom.
      • Tempo and swing: drag up or down, or tap the arrows; hold an arrow to keep going. Tap the middle of Tempo in time with the music to set it.
      • Patterns: 1 to 8 along the top; a dot marks the ones with something in them. While it plays, the next one blinks until the bar ends, then takes over. Hold a number to name the pattern, copy it, share it as a link, or chain it to another (Then play) to make a song.
      • Lead: a second line an octave up, on its own tab, with its own wave and length.
      • Hold a step (a note or a drum): its chance (how often it plays) and its repeats (a roll). Hold a drum's name: its tune, decay, level and length. Lengths up to 32 steps; tracks of different lengths drift against each other.
      • Sounds: presets for the knobs. Hold Random for a whole pattern in a style.
      • Song and MIDI are at the top of this menu: patterns in order; a keyboard, a controller and a clock (in Chrome, Edge or Android). A pattern or the song saves as a MIDI file.
      • VJ (in rave mode): colours, mirrors, trails, scrolling words, Auto VJ, a second screen for a projector, and Rec with the picture.
      • On a computer: a big window shows the bass, the lead, the drums and the punch-ins at once (drag the line under the display to share the space); scroll on a knob or a note to turn it; play notes on the letters A to K; Keys… above lists every shortcut. A pattern or the song saves as audio (with stems) from its menu.
      • Undo: the arrows beside the tabs undo and redo any change to a pattern.
      • Rec: records everything you hear, effects and all; when you stop, save it or send it.
      • The squelch pad stays where you leave it; double-tap it to go back to the start.
      • On a keyboard: Space plays, the number row holds the punch-ins (1 to 0, - and =), Shift with 1 to 8 picks a pattern, ← and → the tempo (with Shift, by 5), R the rave, ⌘Z undo.
      • In Drift, hold a finger on the picture for a note that stays until you let go (two fingers, two notes). Freeze holds everything as it is. Sounds… picks the pad, the bells and up to two textures underneath (rain, wind, the sea, fire, a city, birds, vinyl), a drone and a pulse. Modes…: Sleep, Wake and Focus. Keep…: your favourites (the ★), played in turn if you like, and saving as audio. Dim blacks out the screen while it plays.
      • Drift: an ambient mode that plays itself, never quite repeating. The picture is its mood: drag for dark to bright and sparse to busy; tap it for a bell. The weather turns it from calm (a pad and bells) to a storm (the acid line creeps in, then soft drums). A seed word makes the piece: the same word, the same piece, to share as a link. Rain, vinyl or the sea underneath; a sleep timer; save 2, 5 or 10 minutes as audio.
      • The rave: tap it for the next scene. Rave fills the screen with it; there, drag anywhere to play the filter. With two fingers: swipe left or right for another scene, pinch for more or less squelch (env mod), tap for Latch, swipe down to leave. Hold Scene to pick one. The patterns and the tempo are along the top; Lock makes the picture ignore touches. The controls fade after a few seconds: touch to bring them back.
