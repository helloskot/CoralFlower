Acid Flower

An acid house machine. Program a bass line and a beat, then play the filter with your fingers.

For a full-screen machine with its own icon: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

ACIDFLOWER

How it plays

  • Bass line: drag a note up or down to change it; tap it to rest it. Oct drops or lifts it an octave, Acc accents it (louder, a sharper squelch), Slide glides it into the next note.
  • Drums: tap a step to set or clear it, or drag across steps to paint. Tap a drum’s name to hear it.
  • Squelch pad: cutoff left to right, resonance bottom to top. The strips do the rest: drag up or down.
  • Punch: hold a pad for an effect on the whole mix, let go to drop out; hold several at once. Repeats start on the beat. In rave mode they run along the bottom; on a keyboard, 1 to 0, - and =.
  • Tilt: lean the iPad to play the filter: left and right for the cutoff, towards you and away for the resonance. It takes the way you hold it when you turn it on as the middle; turn it off and on again to re-centre. The picture leans with it.
  • Patterns: 1 to 8 along the top. While it plays, the next one starts at the end of the bar.
  • The rave: tap it for the next scene. Rave fills the screen with it; there, drag anywhere to play the filter.
