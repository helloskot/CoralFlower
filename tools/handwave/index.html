Mood

Handwave runs in the browser. The camera only sees your hands: they're tracked on this device (MediaPipe, loaded the first time), and no picture is shown, kept or sent. Part of CoralFlower.

How to play

It plays by itself, softly. Your hands shape it.

Right hand
Across moves through the chords
Up and down lower and darker, or higher and brighter
Nearer the camera swells it, with a melody and bells
Left hand
Across the texture: rain, the sea, wind, birds
Up and down the space: close and dry, or vast
Nearer the camera the weather: calm, then a storm

No camera? Touch and drag: one finger is your right hand (hold it longer to come nearer), a second finger your left.
