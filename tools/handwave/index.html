About

Handwave is part of CoralFlower and runs in the browser. The camera only sees your hands: they're tracked on this device (MediaPipe, loaded the first time), and no picture is shown, kept or sent.

How to play

  1. Tap a pad. Each one is a chord, and every one is in key: there's no wrong note. Slide across them to move through chords.
  2. Pick a flavour. Triad, 7th, 9th, Sus, Power or Wide changes what kind of chord every pad plays.
  3. Press Play for a soft groove; set the drums and the bass in Groove. The bass follows your chords.
  4. Drift lays a slow pad, bells and a texture (vinyl, rain, the sea, birds) under whatever you play, and keeps drifting on the last chord when you stop.
  5. Hands: turn on the camera (it only sees your hands; you'll see them in neon). Right hand: its height picks the chord (low is I, high is VII); pinch thumb and finger to play, let go to stop. Left hand: how many fingers you hold up picks the flavour (a fist is Triad, an open hand is Wide); raise it to brighten the sound.
  6. Chord memory: tap Rec, play some chords (each goes into the next slot), then Loop to play them back a bar each and jam over them.
