The camera sees

Mood

Handwave runs in the browser. The camera only sees your hands: they're tracked on this device (MediaPipe, loaded the first time), and no picture is shown, kept or sent. Part of CoralFlower.

How to play

It plays by itself: ambient house, dub techno. You shape it, and steer the flight.

Right hand
Across moves through the chords, and turns the sky
Up and down lower and darker, or higher and brighter
Nearer the camera swells it, with an arpeggio
Left hand
Across builds the groove: from no beat to the full beat
Up and down the space: dry, or a deep dub echo
Nearer the camera opens the filter
Face
Turn your head to look around; tilt it to bank; open your mouth to open the sound up
Body
Dance and the groove builds; lean to turn; arms wide for space

No camera? Touch and drag: one finger is your right hand (hold it longer to come nearer), a second finger your left.
