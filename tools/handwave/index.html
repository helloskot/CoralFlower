About

Handwave is part of CoralFlower. The hands are tracked on this device (MediaPipe, loaded the first time); no picture leaves it.

How to play

  1. Tap a pad. Each one is a chord, and every one is in key: there's no wrong note. Slide across them to move through chords.
  2. Pick a flavour. Triad, 7th, 9th, Sus, Power or Wide changes what kind of chord every pad plays.
  3. Press Play for a groove; set the drums and the bass in Groove. The bass follows your chords.
  4. Hands: turn on the camera. Right hand: its height picks the chord (low is I, high is VII); pinch thumb and finger to play, let go to stop. Left hand: how many fingers you hold up picks the flavour (a fist is Triad, an open hand is Wide); raise it to brighten the sound.
  5. Mask: a neon wireframe, a visor or an outline on your face, in the picture and in your videos (the red button records).
  6. Chord memory: tap Rec, play some chords (each goes into the next slot), then Loop to play them back a bar each and jam over them.
